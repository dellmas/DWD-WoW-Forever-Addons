WoW Forever Addon Pack v1.0.10
Updated November 6, 2026: the DWD Profiles addons have been removed.
This archive contains 25 addon folders. Use the latest release for current versions.

1. Close WoW and back up your AddOns and WTF folders.
2. Remove DWD_Profiles, !DWD_Profiles_Loader, and the obsolete DWD_Profiles_Recovery
   folder from Interface/AddOns if present. Leave your WTF saved settings intact.
3. Replace the other included addon folders with their clean copies from AddOns.
4. In CurseForge select World of Warcraft > Forever > Scan addons folder > Scan.
5. Reopen WoW. Each remaining addon saves its own settings; no DWD profile restore
   or named layout loader is required. Enable both Questie and QuestieDB.

The ZIP does not delete or replace your saved settings. Existing preset files may
remain in WTF as inactive data. Disable the legacy DWD desktop updater when using
CurseForge for updates. Historical release feeds have also had the retired addons
removed so they cannot be reinstalled through those feeds.
