WoW Forever Addon Pack v1.0.15
Updated November 6, 2026: the DWD Profiles addons have been removed.
This archive contains 26 addon folders. Use the latest release for current versions.

1. Close WoW and back up your AddOns and WTF folders.
2. Remove DWD_Profiles, !DWD_Profiles_Loader, and the obsolete DWD_Profiles_Recovery
   folder from Interface/AddOns if present. Leave your WTF saved settings intact.
3. Replace the other included addon folders with their clean copies from AddOns.
4. In CurseForge select World of Warcraft > Forever > Scan addons folder > Scan.
5. Reopen WoW. Each remaining addon saves its own settings; no DWD profile restore
   or named layout loader is required. Enable both Questie and QuestieDB.

The ZIP does not delete or replace your saved settings. Existing preset files may
remain in WTF as inactive data. Disable the legacy DWD desktop updater when using
CurseForge for updates. Historical release feeds have also had the retired addons
removed so they cannot be reinstalled through those feeds.

Tooltip Mover 1.1.0-forever.9: tooltip scaling preserves its screen anchor,
config shows current scale and font size, and the quest-text move button has
its own row. Reload after updating, then open /ttmove config. Settings remain
in your WTF folder. CurseForge approval/indexing may delay update detection.

MobCount 0.0.1-forever.23: Total Killed now identifies the creature displayed
by the tooltip. Player/pet tooltips are excluded. Counts are omitted while
Forever hides identity, rather than using an unrelated cached target. Run
/reload after updating. Existing kill totals and settings remain in WTF.

DWD Bagnon Helper 1.0.0-forever.3 opens Bagnon inventory after login and UI
reload. Click X to close it. Map, Escape and bag toggles leave it open, and
changing zones respects the X. Enable it with Bagnon and run /reload after
updating. /dwdbags shows status.

Branding release: all six published DWD addons use the same Dog Water logo
and DWD-prefixed display names. Party XP is named DWD Party XP. Addon folder
names, CurseForge project IDs, Lua code and settings are unchanged. Reload or
restart WoW to refresh addon-list metadata. CurseForge app names/logos can
lag until moderation, refresh or app restart.

Mob Kill Counter 0.0.1-forever.25 counts NPC killing blows reported for your
character, pet, or vehicle. Forever may hide the attacker or victim in some
content, and those deaths are skipped. Run /reload after updating.
