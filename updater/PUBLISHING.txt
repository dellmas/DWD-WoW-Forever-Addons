Release maintenance after the CurseForge transition

Keep DWD-Updates.json and DWD-Only-Addons.zip on every latest release for older
updater installations. The manifest release must equal the GitHub tag.
The manifest may contain a subset of the fixed allowlist. For v1.0.6 it contains
only the five supported folders still present in the full pack. Do not restore
the retired recovery or updater-status helpers just to populate that allowlist.
Party Highlight and RankUp are outside the legacy allowlist; use CurseForge.
Validate the manifest and unpacked checksums with updater.validate_manifest
and updater.unpack_verified before publishing. Do not include any WTF data,
account settings, junctions, credentials, or personal profile seeds.

The full pack uses unmodified CurseForge archives. CURSEFORGE-PROJECTS.json
records their source URLs, archive checksums, folders, and per-file checksums.
